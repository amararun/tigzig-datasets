# NEWS

Headlines and short summary lines from 70+ public RSS feeds of news publishers, central banks and agencies, each scored for relevance to a macro and credit analyst.

Source: Public RSS feeds of news publishers, central banks and agencies (each row names its source).

Browse on tigzig.com: https://www.tigzig.com/downloads#app-news · JSON for this group: https://api.tigzig.com/downloads/news

| Dataset | What it holds | Updated | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| **News headlines (70+ feeds)**<br>[Docs](https://www.tigzig.com/apis/news) | One row per headline since 25 Oct 2026: publisher time, source, feed, category, title, link to the publisher, the feed's own summary line, a 0 to 1 relevance score, and a duplicate marker. The whole history is in one file, rebuilt daily. | daily | [Parquet](https://api.tigzig.com/news/v1/download/news_headlines.parquet) · [CSV.gz](https://api.tigzig.com/news/v1/download/news_headlines.csv.gz) |

Provided as is, with no guarantee of accuracy, completeness or availability.
